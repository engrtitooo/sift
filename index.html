S Sift

Your screenshots, sorted.

Sift reads the screenshots already on your phone — the tickets, the addresses, the Wi-Fi passwords, the things you meant to buy — and turns each one into something you can act on. What it reads stays on your phone.

Privacy Policy Support

What it does

  • Finds the screenshots in your photo library and leaves your camera photos alone.
  • Reads each one and gives you a title, a summary and the useful details — a date, a place, a price, a link, a code.
  • Offers one action per screenshot: add to calendar, save the place, track the product, set a reminder, keep a note, or delete the picture.
  • Never deletes a photo without asking you first, every single time.

How it handles your data

There is no account and no sign-up. A screenshot is shrunk on your phone, sent to be read, and discarded the moment the answer comes back — no copy is kept on any server. What comes back is stored on your phone only. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
