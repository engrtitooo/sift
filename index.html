Your screenshots, sorted.

Sift reads the screenshots already on your phone — the tickets, the addresses, the Wi-Fi passwords, the things you meant to buy — and turns each one into something you can act on. What it reads stays on your phone.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Support

What it does

  • Finds the screenshots in your photo library and leaves your camera photos alone.
  • Reads each one and gives you a title, a summary and the useful details — a date, a place, a price, a link, a code.
  • Offers one action per screenshot: add to calendar, save the place, track the product, set a reminder, keep a note, or delete the picture.
  • Never deletes a photo without asking you first, every single time.

How it handles your data

There is no account and no sign-up. A screenshot is shrunk on your phone and sent to our server and Google's Gemini AI to be read, then discarded — our servers never keep a copy. What comes back is stored on your phone only. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Works in your language

English, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese and French, with full right-to-left support for Arabic. Sift writes titles and summaries in the language you choose, whatever language the screenshot is in.

Need help?

Restoring a purchase, cancelling a subscription, or asking us to delete what we hold: the Support page covers it, or write to eaeltayb@gmail.com.
